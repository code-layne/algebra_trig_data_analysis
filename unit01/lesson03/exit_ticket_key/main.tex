\begin{notesbox}{Factoring: GCF and Grouping --- On Your Own}
\small
Notes closed. Three items. Factor completely, and check each answer by multiplying back.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=6pt]

\item Factor completely: $18x^3-24x^2$
\begin{work}
  18x^3-24x^2 &= 6x^2(3x) - 6x^2(4) \\
              &= 6x^2(3x-4)
\end{work}

\item Factor by grouping: $x^3+7x^2+2x+14$
\begin{work}
  x^3+7x^2+2x+14 &= x^2(x+7) + 2(x+7) \\
                 &= (x+7)\left(x^2+2\right)
\end{work}

\item A student factors $20x^2+30x$ as $5x(4x+6)$. Is the answer correct? Is it complete? Explain
in one sentence, then give the complete factorization.
\begin{work}
  20x^2+30x &= 10x(2x) + 10x(3) \\
            &= 10x(2x+3)
\end{work}
\ansline{Correct but not complete --- $4x+6$ still shares a $2$; the GCF is $10x$, not $5x$.}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
